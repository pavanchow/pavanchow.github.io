How to Use E-book Creator

Welcome to the E-book Creator! Follow these steps to create your e-book:

1. Book Title, Author & Cover:

  • Start by entering your Book Title.
  • Enter the Author Name (for Title Page). This name will appear on the title page byline.
  • Optionally, upload a Cover Image (JPEG or PNG recommended). This will be used as the EPUB cover.

2. Book Structure:

  • Next, go to the "Book Structure" section. Here you can:
    • Add new Custom Text Sections.
    • Reorder all sections (including Table of Contents, predefined, and custom) using "Up" and "Down" buttons.
    • Show or Hide optional predefined sections (Preface, About Author, Appendix, Index) and the Table of Contents. Hidden sections won't appear in your e-book.
    • Rename your custom sections.
    • Delete custom sections.
  • After setting up the structure, use "Next" and "Back" to move through your defined sections to add content.
  • The "Current Step" indicator at the top shows where you are.
  • You can jump directly to the export options using the "Skip to Export" button.

3. Entering Content:

  • Each section (Preface, About Author, Custom Sections, Chapters, etc.) has a dedicated input area. The Table of Contents section is auto-generated and not directly editable.
  • Simply type or paste your content into the text boxes. The "About the Author" section is where you can provide a detailed biography, including images.

4. Managing Chapters (within a "Chapters" section):

  • When you are editing a "Chapters" section:
  • Enter a Chapter Title and its Content.
  • Click "Save and Add Another Chapter" to save the current chapter and start a new one within that "Chapters" block.
  • Use "Next Chapter" (if available) or "Next Section" to move on.

5. Formatting with Markdown:

You can use Markdown syntax in all content areas for rich formatting. Here are some common examples:

  • Headings: # Heading 1, ## Heading 2, ### Heading 3
  • Emphasis: *italic* or _italic_, **bold** or __bold__
  • Lists: Unordered: - Item 1; Ordered: 1. Item 1
  • Links: [Link Text](https://example.com)
  • Images: Use the "Add Image" button (alt text will be "Image") or Markdown ![Alt Text](image_url).
  • Tables, Code Blocks, Blockquotes/Prompts are also supported.

6. Previewing:

  • Click the "Preview E-book" button at any time.

7. Exporting:

  • Navigate or skip to the "Finish & Export" section.
  • Choose your format (.txt, .html, .pdf, .epub).
  • Click "Export E-book".
  • For .mobi/.azw, export as .epub and use a tool like Calibre.

Happy e-book writing!
